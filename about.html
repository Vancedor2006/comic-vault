About Comic-Vault

Comic-Vault is a home for webcomics where every series is read exactly the way its creator intended.

Creators choose a reading direction when they publish, and Comic-Vault sticks to it, so a manga always reads right to left and a webtoon always scrolls.

Three ways to read

Vertical Scroll

One long strip you scroll down, like webtoons. Made for phones.

Manga (RTL)

Page by page, right to left. Tap or swipe the way a manga turns.

Western (LTR)

Page by page, left to right, like most western comics.

For readers

  • No account needed to read. Comic-Vault remembers where you stopped (in your browser) and shows it under Continue reading.
  • On phones, swipe to turn pages and tap the middle of a page to hide the controls.
  • Pick a light or dark theme, a reader background, and two-page spreads on big screens.
  • Add Comic-Vault to your home screen to open it like an app.

For creators

  • Publishing is free: create an account, then go to Publish.
  • Upload pages as images or as a ZIP file. Large images are optimised automatically so they load fast.
  • Fix typos, reorder pages, replace your cover or delete things from My Comics.
  • Please read the terms & creator rules before uploading.

Contact

Found a bug, have an idea, or need to report a comic that breaks the rules? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page and include a link to the page involved.
