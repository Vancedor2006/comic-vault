Terms & creator rules

The short version: only upload comics you have the right to share, be decent, and remember this is a small community project.

Using Comic-Vault

  • Anyone can read comics without an account.
  • You need an account to publish. Keep your password safe; you're responsible for what's posted from your account.
  • Comic-Vault is provided as-is, without guarantees that it will always be available or free of bugs.

What you can upload

  • Only work you created, or have permission to publish. Don't upload other people's comics, scans or art.
  • You keep the rights to your work. By publishing, you allow Comic-Vault to store it and show it to readers.
  • No illegal content, no sexual content involving minors, no harassment or hate targeting people for who they are, and no spam or malware.
  • Content that breaks these rules may be removed and the account suspended.

Removing your work

You can edit or delete any chapter or series you published at any time from My Comics. Deleting removes the pages from Comic-Vault's storage.

Your data

  • Your account stores your email address, username and the comics you publish.
  • Reading progress, theme and reader settings are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to Comic-Vault, and clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Accounts and files are hosted by Supabase.

Reporting a problem

To report content that breaks these rules, see Contact.
